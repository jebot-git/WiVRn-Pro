Session microphone keep-alive on the pre-upstream-merge Pro build

Base: ed6325b6c427ca47d64b0ef6f3ccfb4ac5e22cdb, preserving local Pro
encoding, reliability, tracking and dashboard changes. No full upstream merge.

During a visible VR session, permitted and enabled microphone capture stays open
indefinitely, including before the first PC reader and throughout world-loading
gaps. PipeWire reader changes control forwarding only. Without a reader, headset
callbacks discard samples locally; no idle microphone audio is transmitted.
PC default input, PipeWire routing, application mute and voice activation remain
unchanged. Existing Quest processed/unprocessed microphone selection is preserved;
there is no new amplitude gate, noise filter, or silence-triggered recovery.

Audio settings provide Microphone capture and Restart microphone controls.
Permission loss, reported Android system microphone mute, Activity pause, session invisibility,
and disconnect stop capture. Read-only permission/mute checks run every 250 ms. Android enforces its own
privacy toggle; its state is not exposed by the public SensorPrivacyManager API.
Presence loss suppresses delivery and recovery while retaining an otherwise
allowed stream. Returning presence forces a fresh stream. Activity pause/resume
epochs also force reacquisition, including transitions shorter than the polling
interval. Visibility is the fallback on runtimes without presence events.

Android lifecycle operations run on one owner thread. Callbacks only enqueue
bounded samples when forwarding is requested. Reader and stream generations
reject retired buffers, and samples older than 100 ms are discarded. No network
send occurs in the audio callback. AAudio uses shared capture and preserves the
existing VOICE_COMMUNICATION default and optional UNPROCESSED input preset.
After a fault, compatibility capture requests performance mode NONE and switches
processed capture to VOICE_RECOGNITION. Explicit UNPROCESSED selection remains
unprocessed. The compatibility choice lasts for the streaming session and may
change voice processing. It does not alter global Android audio mode or mute.

Recovery checks callback cadence and AAudio timestamps, never PCM amplitude.
A three-second stall or delivery below half the negotiated sample rate permits
three initial reopen attempts with backoff, then at most one attempt every 30
seconds while present. Ten seconds of healthy progress, reactivation, or manual
restart rearms fast recovery. Unsupported timestamps alone are not a fault.
Off-head and system-muted states do not spend recovery retries. Every close is
followed by at least one second before reacquisition; resume also waits one second
for Android routing to settle. The owner thread remains responsive during this
interval. Manual restart immediately releases the input and selects compatibility
capture without restarting the VR session or bypassing capture permission/mute.

Quest HAL contention can produce synthetic silence and advancing timestamps.
This design avoids consumer-driven reopen cycles; it cannot guarantee hardware
priority over Meta services or detect every silent HAL failure. Hardware testing
of this candidate is required, including long world loads, system mute, presence,
suspend/resume, noise suppression and disconnect. Build validation is recorded
alongside the candidate artifacts; this document does not claim deployment.

2026-10-07 regression: after wake at 05:17:18, negotiated 48 kHz input delivered
about 13-14 kHz with advancing timestamps. Three automatic and three manual
reopens of the same VOICE_COMMUNICATION route failed. The retry/profile/lifecycle
changes above target this observed failure; a headset sleep/wake test is still
required to establish whether the vendor HAL recovers with the new profile.
