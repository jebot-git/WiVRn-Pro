EXACT FULL-DEADLINE COUNT CHECK -- 2026-10-09

Result
The promising loose supply/phase ceiling does not survive a requirement to
serve every observed warm deadline with a new advancing wire frame. On run05,
the exact maximum count strictly under 15ms is 184 of 1,900 at original times,
and 26 of 1,900 after a +5.555555ms sampling shift. Hypothetical publication
advances up to 8ms do not change those full-count maxima in the tested cases.

The earlier good-only path had 1,805 good choices at +5.555555ms / 4ms advance,
which met a necessary 95% count condition. It did not establish that the other
95 choices could be inserted while preserving wire/target order. This branch
solves that joint constraint exactly rather than interpreting the ceiling as
an attainable 1,900-choice result.

This remains an optimistic known-supply model, not a live pacing result. It
does not change native behavior, production or research selection policies,
qualification limits, original evidence or headset suspension.

Exact algorithm and proof boundary
full_count.py requires exactly one choice at EVERY fixed warm deadline. Frames
must have exact known metadata, be ready at the hypothetical selection time,
strictly increase in wire ID and not regress in source target. The dynamic
program keeps the best good count for each ending frame after each deadline.
Only the previous deadline's states enter the prefix-maximum structure, so it
cannot skip a deadline or select twice at one deadline. Histories that end at
the same frame have identical future feasibility; retaining their best score
is therefore sufficient.

For D required choices from N sorted known identities, the zero-based frame
rank chosen at deadline k lies in [k, N-D+k]. At least k identities must precede
it and D-k-1 must follow it. This necessary rank band is independent of target
or readiness constraints. It reduces run05's working band to 195 frames and
keeps about 360,000 reachable states per scenario. A five-million-candidate
rank-band budget fails explicitly rather than silently pruning more states.
Compact parent arrays retain exact backtracking certificates.

The objective is maximum under-threshold count at this mandatory total count.
Every certificate is rechecked for frame/target order, publication readiness
and score. Its age percentiles describe that certificate only: ties can choose
older frames, so those percentiles are NOT claimed to be minimum possible
percentiles. The threshold feasibility conclusion uses the exact maximum good
count and nearest-rank required good count, not a percentile of a cherry-picked
or filtered sample population.

Independent validation
full-count-validation-20261009.json binds 259 passing Python tests. The new
layered solver matches exhaustive combinations on 512 small random populations
with nonmonotonic target order, readiness constraints, unavailable frames and
varying mandatory counts. A specific 20-deadline regression has a 19-good
skipping path (enough for the loose 95% ceiling), yet requiring all 20 distinct
choices forces zero good choices. This catches the exact inference error the
new solver is intended to prevent.

The transport team independently reviewed the rank-band proof, previous-layer
only prefix states, score dominance, receipt/readiness ordering, certificate
recheck and nearest-rank threshold. It found no blocker.

The surprising +5.555555ms / 4ms case was also recomputed with the earlier
generic DAG/Fenwick solver, which permits skips and maximizes unique count
first, good count second. full-count-reference-20261009.json records 361,132
candidate nodes, a maximum 1,900-choice chain, and the same exact good count
26. This is a different optimization formulation, with the same admitted
known-supply assumptions. Both analyses bind source and immutable input hashes.

Interpretation and next boundary
This full-count requirement is stronger than the existing 88Hz cadence floor.
Failure at all 1,900 deadlines does not by itself rule out an alternative that
uses slightly fewer distinct choices while retaining acceptable cadence. The
next exact question is the count/age frontier with a small explicit skip
budget. That investigation must preserve cadence evidence and continue to
separate hypothetical supply/phase changes from executable runtime behavior.
