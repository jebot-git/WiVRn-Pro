Necessary endpoint cadence/age envelope, 2026-10-09

This approach removes the sufficient full-window count assumption for a
bounded set of five declared phases. It does not solve mixed-age path
feasibility or change the native policy. The original run05 failed summary,
all 300 manifest-bound files and pinned manifest identity remain unchanged.
No headset operation or performance measurement is performed.

For each of four admission models and each listed phase, the analyzer
examines all 1,804,050 pairs of observed warm deadlines. Exactly 509,001
intervals span at least 10 seconds, and all of those have enough observed
deadlines for the 88 Hz count floor. Shorter-than-10-second intervals fail
that declared duration requirement and are reported separately.

For an endpoint span S, any path meeting 88 Hz requires at least
Nmin = max(180, ceil(88*S/1e9)+1) choices. Nearest-rank p95 strictly below
15 ms then needs at least ceil(0.95*Nmin) strictly good choices. Deleting
bad-age choices from any legal path preserves deadline and identity order,
so its good count cannot exceed the exact longest good-only advancing
chain within this interval. The analyzer computes that chain length with
patience/LIS tails: each deadline's alternative identities enter in descending
order, preventing two choices at the same deadline; bisect_left enforces
strict identity advance. Checked global monotone frame targets also preserve
target nonregression. Source-distinct generations were checked contiguous
and ordered over all known encoded frames before warm-prefix filtering.

This is a necessary upper bound, not an executable selector. It ignores
whether the remaining bad-age choices can be inserted to reach Nmin,
whether the chosen endpoints themselves can be used, and real handle
ownership. A negative maximum margin rules out every interval for that
fixed phase/model. Zero or positive margins are inconclusive, not paths.

Every participating eye is optimistically ready at its own original receipt.
Encoded age uses the original minimum stereo receipt. Runtime targets and
actual pre-warm identity exclusions stay fixed; known metadata may be used
before it was first observed. Earlier network receipt, unseen supply,
runtime feedback, optics and changed warm membership are not modeled.
A rejection for encoded receipt age also rejects the pointwise older known
source receipt age within the same model. Source identity is not an input epoch.
