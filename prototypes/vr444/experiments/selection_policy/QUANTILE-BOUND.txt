Exact optimistic p95 bounds over the finite positive-phase grid
=============================================================

Status: host-only offline mathematical model. No headset operations, native
behavior changes, performance promotion or release-gate evaluation. This branch
builds on 7876b29 and reuses both preserved validated exact C++ solvers unchanged.

Results
-------
For 1,875 choices over the fixed warm window, 8ms hypothetical per-eye publication
advance, and the 25 tested phases from 0 through 11.111111ms:

  Selection identity / target ceiling       Best tested phase   Minimum p95
  Encoded IDs / one bounded-newest horizon      0ms             19.241095ms
  Encoded IDs / no ceiling                     +4ms             14.874793ms
  Distinct source sets / two horizons          +5.5ms           15.936408ms
  Distinct source sets / no ceiling            +5.5ms           15.936408ms

These values ARE exact minimum nearest-rank p95 within each declared finite
model. They are not merely the p95 of a certificate optimized for the count below
15ms. The distinct-source two-horizon and unbounded quantile curves coincide at
all 25 phases in this acquisition. A rendered figure is included, with discrete
sample points and explicit finite-grid/ownership limitations.

The discontinuities matter. In the encoded/unbounded model the minimum p95 drops
from 19.470626ms at +3.5ms phase to 14.874793ms at +4ms phase. In the distinct-source
model it drops from 20.531824ms at +5ms phase to 15.936408ms at +5.5ms. Fixed-order
count constraints and eligibility changes can force different paths; subtracting
a stage median from end-to-end age would not predict these changes. This does not
establish the causes or removability of actual scheduling delay.

Why one count and the largest declared publication advance suffice here
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The 21.284347901-second warm span makes 1,875 unique choices sufficient for the
88Hz cadence reference; the audited certificates also calculate actual endpoint
cadence. Counts 1,875 through 1,900 were the preceding model's allowed range.
Any path with a larger count can delete its worst-age choices down to 1,875,
preserving strict frame/source/deadline order, nonregressing targets, readiness
and warm-prefix constraints. Removing the largest ages cannot worsen nearest-rank
p95. The retained count still suffices for 88Hz over the original full span.
Therefore the minimum p95 at 1,875 also gives the minimum over that allowed count
range. This does not cover lower counts using a shorter endpoint interval.

Increasing the hypothetical publication advance only reduces the ready timestamp
(clipped at each eye's actual receipt); it does not change receipt age, target or
identity. The 8ms advance has an eligibility superset of the earlier 0/1/2/4ms
advances, so its optimum cannot be worse. Eight milliseconds remains a declared
hypothesis, not a measured removable decode cost or proof of instantaneous decode.

Exact integer search and certificates
-------------------------------------
At fixed count K, the unchanged solver maximizes the number of ages strictly less
than integer threshold T. Nearest-rank p95 requires ceil(95*K/100) such ages. The
first passing T therefore gives the exact attainable quantile q95=T-1ns.

minimum_p95 starts from an independently audited certificate at 15ms. Its actual
p95 supplies a feasible upper bound; bisection then finds the first passing
integer threshold. Every queried solver frontier is independently audited for
counts, ordering, original receipt/readiness, target ceilings and, where used,
actual source-generation identities and warm-up exclusion. All queried optimum
scores must be monotone in T. The final chosen path has p95 exactly T-1, while the
adjacent threshold T-1 has too few fresh choices. Threshold zero is analytically
failing because every admitted age is nonnegative. Both boundary scores and all
intermediate certificate hashes are retained. Best-phase full certificates are
kept; other cells retain exact hashes and query boundaries for reproduction.

The fixed hypothetical population is also hashed per cell. No interpolation or
continuous-phase optimality is claimed. Original runtime targets stay fixed under
selection/submission shifts. Unlimited retention and earlier use of later-observed
metadata remain optimistic assumptions; actual handle ownership, runtime feedback,
projection quality and upper admission are unqualified. Source identities do not
measure simulation/input epochs. No host/client clock subtraction is performed.
