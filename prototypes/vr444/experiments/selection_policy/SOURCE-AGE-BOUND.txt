Known-source receipt-age optimum brackets, 2026-10-09

SOURCE-RECEIPT-AGE rescored existing paths but did not optimize its separate
metric. This follow-up derives rigorous lower and upper bounds without
claiming a new exact source-age optimizer.

For every known encoded frame, the earliest known receipt of its canonical
application-source set is no later than that frame's own receipt. Therefore
known-source age is at least encoded age, component by component, on every
legal path and phase. Nearest-rank p95 is monotone under such increases.
The exact global encoded-age minimum is consequently a lower bound on the
source-age minimum over the same feasible set. Independently rescoring one
legal witness supplies an upper bound. The code verifies receipt dominance
across the entire known population, not only the selected witness.

Model                           minimum known-source p95 interval, ms
  encoded, one target horizon              [19.241095, 19.311512]
  encoded, no target ceiling               [14.852189, 14.974637]
  distinct source, two horizons            [15.924690, 15.998856]
  distinct source, no target ceiling       [15.924690, 15.998856]

All endpoints are inclusive bounds. None of these intervals collapses to
one value, so no exact source-age optimum is reported. The legal upper-bound
witness phases are respectively 0, 4.387188, 5.488282 and 5.488282 ms.

Under this declared model, a strict source-age p95 below 15 ms is impossible
for the one-horizon encoded model and for both source-distinct models. The
unbounded-target encoded model has a legal witness below 15 ms even after
known-source age is used (14.974637 ms). That witness still permits repeated
source generations, unlimited retention and later-observed information.
Neither verdict is a headset or optical-latency claim.

The scope is unchanged from EXACT-PHASE: every integer-nanosecond common
selection/submit shift from 0 through 11,111,111, zero receive-to-publication
delay clipped to each participating eye's original receipt, fixed runtime
target sequence, fixed warm membership and actual pre-warm identity history,
and the sufficient 1,875-choice count for 88 Hz over the full warm span.
Earlier physical receipts, unseen frames, native ownership/feedback, and
smaller counts over shorter endpoint spans remain outside these bounds.
