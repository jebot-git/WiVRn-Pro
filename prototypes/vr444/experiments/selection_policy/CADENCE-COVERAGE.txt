The native cadence metric counts global first encoded-frame uses after
warmup and divides count minus one by the interval between the first and
last such use. The exact-phase bounds instead required enough frames for
88 Hz over the full observed warm span. That latter count is sufficient,
not necessary when a candidate's first/last-new-frame endpoints differ.

The new analyzer independently reconstructs global first-use membership,
including pre-warm history, and checks its count/span against native_metrics.
It reports the observed prefix/suffix without a new frame, endpoint coverage,
full-observation transition rate, observed gaps, and age of every presented
warm stereo frame. Age uses the minimum stereo receipt in the same headset
clock. The gap between warm cutoff and first observed warm submission is
separate: absent captured rows are not labeled repeated-frame holds. With
no new frames, prefix and suffix cover the same span and must not be added.
Missing process tails, input novelty and optical latency remain unknown.
