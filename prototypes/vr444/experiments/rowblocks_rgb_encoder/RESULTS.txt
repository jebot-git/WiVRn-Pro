Avoiding an intermediate representation alone does not prove an optimized path.
Scalar three-byte stores are a possible remaining cost, not an isolated cause
established by this experiment.

Packed RRB32 four-worker retained algorithm-owned vector capacities range
711,760..846,308 bytes for captured pairs. This excludes source/oracle storage,
allocator overhead, thread stacks and transient peaks. The representation removes
the redundant encoder RGB buffer and uses RGB rather than RGBA band scratch;
that structural storage reduction is not a process-RSS or latency guarantee.
