OBSERVED STEREO TIMELINE -- 2026-10-09

This branch decomposes the first successful stereo submissions in all five
preserved CPU captures. It validates runtime frame/warp/eye joins, timestamp
order, stable repeats and all 1,492 manifest entries before deriving metrics.
The 2-second warmup and exact first-submission rules match the existing metrics.
No original summary changes. Runs 01--04 retain their original pass status;
run05 retains its original failed diagnostic status, with separate trace
reinterpretation from the prior repair branch.

Each sample is decomposed exactly into:
  earliest eye receipt -> complete stereo publication,
  publication -> end of selection,
  end of selection -> successful xrEndFrame return,
  return -> the older post-submit log observation.
The first interval includes transport completion, queueing, decode, upload,
evidence I/O and publication locking. It is not a pure decoder time. Decoder
subinterval distributions are joined to the same warm selected frame/eye IDs
but remain separate because they overlap/nest.

Interpretation limits
Most observed selected-frame age accumulated after publication, but causes and
removability remain unresolved. Earlier decode/arrival can change which frame
is eligible at a deadline, causing discrete effects rather than a simple sum
of stage savings. Waiting may reflect intentional target alignment, ownership,
render-loop cadence or other dependencies. No measured speedup, causal blame,
new native behavior, optical measurement or latency gate is asserted here.

The original decoder files have no terminal worker summaries because the app
was terminated. That does not invalidate the already observed, individually
joined selected frame rows, but full decoder/process-tail accounting is NOT
verified. The report exposes terminal-summary presence and always leaves
process-tail verification false. No current qualification policy is relaxed.
