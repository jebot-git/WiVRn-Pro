VWG1 exact-byte and matched-erasure qualification, 2026-10-09
============================================================

23 protocol tests pass, including every source-record loss in the bounded test
fixture, one loss per window, two source losses, source plus parity loss, 500
record mutations, full eye withholding, arbitrary block order, maximum block
count, handle/deadline/configuration binding, fixed payload caps, and permanent
sender abort. The peer-reviewed canonical parity-length ordering is covered by

before it can publish stereo. A separate original-final-source regression drops
the redundant second manifest: known overlong or invalid-recovery parity must
reject before the intact right-eye source can publish. Its valid-parity control
passes. Fresh expiry during recovered decoding also passes.

Matched-erasure results reveal the recovery cost
-----------------------------------------------

The comparison erases identical consecutive CODEC DATA identities, choosing
first/middle/last starts and lengths 1/2/4/8. All manifests and parity remain.
This is not an equal contiguous on-wire burst or an assumed loss distribution.

Across 1728 captured two-format cases:
  Erased identities   Cases    VWG complete    VXP complete
          1             432        432             432
          2             432         38             301
          4             432          0             223
          8             432          0             165

456 captured cases complete in both formats, 665 only with per-block repair,
14 only with packet-window repair, and 593 with neither. Across all 3180
captured+synthetic cases, the corresponding counts are 862 / 1218 / 17 / 1083.
Every successful result was exact; failed candidates expired without publication.
The source grouping therefore trades a substantial reduction in repair bytes
for much weaker recovery in this declared multi-fragment-erasure matrix.

Maximum observed combined retained payload was 409750 bytes. The conservative
1741488-byte theoretical bound includes two repair windows plus inner assembly;
it is not peak RSS and excludes temporary copies/previously published pairs.

No format is promoted. Physical link behavior, congestion, authenticated handle
negotiation, actual server/transfer/client overlap, device support and original
headset receipt-to-post-xrEndFrame latency remain untested. This branch preserves
the exact alternative and its measured byte/recovery tradeoff for later selection.
