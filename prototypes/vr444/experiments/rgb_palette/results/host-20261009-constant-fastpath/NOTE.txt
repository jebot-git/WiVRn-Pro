Same-format constant-tile fast path, 2026-10-09

Constant tiles now bypass palette/RLE candidate allocation/scans in the encoder,
and fill output rows directly in the decoder. Sanitizer coverage is unchanged and
passes. All 38 case/profile wire sizes exactly match the initial benchmark.

31 repetitions, three warmups; CPU measurement window coordinated with the other
teams. Per-case medians were averaged within each group; no pooled p95 claim.

tradeoff, not a promoted or end-to-end latency improvement. Original results are

state. Repeated improvements do not establish an Android speedup.
