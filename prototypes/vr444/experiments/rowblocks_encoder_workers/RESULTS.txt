The new serial staging costs more than the existing one-eye-at-a-time reference.
An inference worth testing is that retaining both full decoded images and separate
per-block workspaces introduces cache/staging overhead. The current data does not
isolate that mechanism. The next distinct candidate can fuse original independent
VR444 tile-band reconstruction and row-block encoding in each bounded worker job,
preserving exact RRB output while avoiding the two global-stage organization.
