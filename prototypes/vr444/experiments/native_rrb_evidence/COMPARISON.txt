Submitted-image comparison adapter

validation/wivrn-codecs/integration/submitted_comparison.py joins three explicitly
SHA256-pinned audit receipts: native submitted capture, independent RLP source
job, and actual-device replay collector. The collector-pinned replay plan binds
uploaded job and shader digests; the comparator also reads actual job/shader,
projection, mask, canonical candidate and retained reference bytes. Every read
is hash-bound and rechecked after comparison. Explicit replay_root confines
absolute as well as relative collector product paths; links/escapes fail.

The standalone native and embedded job audits must match all semantic capture,
run, build and renderer fields. Job input supersets preserve every native input;
only the job's two declared additional build-source dependencies are permitted.
Both eyes are required for the selected capture/frame/warp. Physical identity
relies on the explicit-hash upstream collector's direct-serial or pinned-campaign
bridge audit. This tool does not rerun the acquisition auditors or devices.

The projection mask is independently recomputed from the captured actual FOV
and viewport; saved masks must match it exactly. Only declared RGBA8/BGRA8 byte
permutation is allowed, with no color transformation. Protected RGB tolerance
is exactly0. Full RGB equality and alpha equality/opacity are separately
reported. A one-code protected RGB difference fails even with repaired hashes.

Historical VIEW capture1: both69,612-pixel masks are exact; full RGB and alpha
arrays are also identical. The source boundary is POST-HOST-FOVEATION input.
Native geometry is reconstructed from metadata, not captured VBO/push bytes;
AArch64 geometry bit-equivalence remains unverified. This is a component pixel
comparison, not original-app preservation, runtime timewarp/display visibility,
all-fixture/repeat quality qualification, or a latency result. Prior physical
black feedback remains valid. Every output keeps full_goal_qualified=false.

CLI (all receipt SHAs explicit; plan SHA comes from the pinned collector):
  python3 validation/wivrn-codecs/integration/submitted_comparison.py
    --native-audit NATIVE_AUDIT --native-audit-sha256 SHA
    --job-audit JOB_AUDIT --job-audit-sha256 SHA
    --replay-audit REPLAY_AUDIT --replay-audit-sha256 SHA
    --replay-plan PLAN --replay-root EXPLICIT_PRODUCT_ROOT --out NEW_JSON

Ten test groups cover both channel orders, missing/aliased right output,
identity/device/source/build/hash mutations, repaired control hashes, geometry
and mask changes, symlink/escape rejection, valid absolute paths, legitimate
build dependency supersets, one-code failure and separate alpha reporting.
Read-only compositor peer review found no remaining material blocker after
the absolute-path and build-input-superset compatibility fixes.
