Research basis and bounded hypothesis

Local measured motivation
validation/results/native-codec-comparison-0.12.0/BOTTLENECKS.txt records custom
client decode/upload medians 8.248 and 7.613 ms versus GPU dispatch 4.719 and
4.221 ms in the two custom runs. LZ4 decode was about 0.021 ms. The current
compact-to-expanded representation is 1,843,200 slot bytes per 576x640 eye,
versus 1,474,560 output RGBA bytes. These observations motivate testing elimination
of expansion and GPU inverse; they do not predict live CPU backend latency.

Wire/reference implementation (primary local sources)
.worktrees/wivrn-codec-tests/common/codec_test/vr444/codec.cpp
.worktrees/wivrn-codec-tests/common/codec_test/vr444/vulkan_runner.cpp
The compact grammar and exact CPU decode are the oracle. The experimental
constant shortcut uses the same canonical AC-mask condition as the reviewed
coop128dc decoder; every one of the 48 active AC positions has a negative fixture.
This design changes neither coefficient quantization nor reconstructed pixels.

Primary architecture reference
Arm Neon Intrinsics Reference:
https://arm-software.github.io/acle/neon_intrinsics/advsimd.html
Consulted 2026-10-08. AArch64 load/store, vector add/subtract, signed arithmetic
right shift and zip intrinsics support the exact integer lifting implementation.
The arithmetic shift supplies floor-half for negative detail coefficients, and
zip restores interleaved samples. This reference establishes operation semantics;
it does not promise performance on the Quest SoC. On-device timing and exactness
are required. No claim about Vulkan driver performance is inferred from it.

Decision
Implement compact direct scalar decode plus the same parser with NEON inverse.
Avoid changing protocol, neural reconstruction or tile quantization in this probe:
those introduce separate quality and integration variables without explaining the
measured decode-stage overhead. RGBA upload and scheduling remain explicit later
gates. No optical motion-to-photon claim is possible from this standalone harness.
