VXB1 qualification, 2026-10-09
=============================

20 protocol tests passed, including 500 deterministic record-bit mutations,
CRC-repaired invalid LZ4, odd/partial blocks, 512-block stereo completion, whole
stereo withholding, valid descriptor/manifest conflicts, orphan expiry, epoch
reset, receiver-issued tickets, and fresh expiry during final assembly.

The final immutable-input qualification passed:
  104 profile/block-size cases (26 stereo profiles x 4 row sizes)
   94 eligible cases; 10 over-cap cases rejected before whole-pair emission
  658 logical delivery replays (94 x 7 scenarios)
  376 exact canonical RRB stereo publications (four successful scenarios)
  282 whole-stereo withholds (DATA lost, corrupted or truncated)
   94 native ASan/UBSan both-eye VR444/RGBA/RRB byte-oracle invocations
    5 native malformed-input cases rejected without success output or sanitizer
      diagnostics: each eye corrupt, right truncation, source shape above pixel
      cap, and right-eye RGBA oracle corruption.

Corpus: 24 actual captured eyes forming 12 matched stereo pairs, plus 14 synthetic
profiles explicitly duplicated across the eyes. All four row sizes are included.
All source, input, binary, compiler-receipt and export-manifest hashes matched
before/after. The committed qualification.json retains per-case/scenario results
and those hashes. Build commands and compiler-discovered dependencies are bound
in build-receipt.json. No headset or socket operations were performed.

These UDP payload totals include two manifests and every DATA header. They omit
IP/link overhead; per-case declared IPv4 byte totals add 28 bytes per record.
RRB canonical headers/tables are reconstructed, not transmitted as DATA. Small
independent blocks therefore increase record/framing costs despite early
availability. No CPU/GPU/network overlap or throughput advantage was measured.

Maximum observed retained pending payload was 245640 bytes in this corpus.
The theoretical 524368-byte retained payload cap is not peak RSS: temporary
joined/assembled copies, prior publication and Python metadata are additional.
Peer review found no bounds/publication blocker and prompted this clarification.

This approach establishes a bounded, exact byte interface for a future streaming
pipeline. A source block lost without recovery prevents full stereo completion.
The incremental sender can discover a later over-budget block and permanently
abort the frame; it does not silently switch content/format or partially publish.
Actual overlap, compression/transport contention, GPU consumption, headset
capability, live deadlines and end-to-end latency remain untested.
