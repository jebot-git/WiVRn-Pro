Common contracts
Codec axes<=4096 and<=4,194,304pixels PER EYE are not the transport capacity.
VXF/VXI/VXB/VXP/VWG preserve<=262144 canonical encoded bytes for the COMPLETE
stereo pair; high-entropy fallback can exceed this and must reject or use an
explicitly qualified alternative, never silently lower quality/enlarge bounds.
Checked immutable inputs and private both-eye staging remain owned until all CPU
workers/GPU consumers complete. Session/config/frame/warp/deadline identity and
fresh complete-stereo publication are native outer obligations.

Existing headset gate is earliest frame receipt to post-xrEndFrame, not optical
latency. This branch's CPU result starts with complete encoded stereo buffers;
its network probe reports sender RTT and receiver-local assembly. Neither can be
added to host encode/GPU/compositor quantiles or promoted as sub15.
