Saved-payload audit adapter:
 python3 integration/rrb_payload_audit.py CAPTURE_DIR AUDIT.json \
   --reference PINNED_SCALAR_BINARY --reference-sha256 SHA256 --source q1|vr444
 Root must bind the chosen binary to rrb_reference.cpp/dependencies in its build
 receipt. The adapter executes no device/network commands, reconstructs exact
 hashed saved payloads sequentially, validates envelope identity/CRC and actual
 admitted pair cap, and compares scalar output with source/reference/client bytes.
 It has a120-second total oracle execution budget and <=128 savedeye payloads.
 Use its status only with run-local capture/provenance qualification; it does not
 authenticate acquisition or establish final-render/display quality by itself.
