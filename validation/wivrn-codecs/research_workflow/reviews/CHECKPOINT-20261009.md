## Remaining constraints

**Wire admission:** the experimental cap remains **262,144 canonical stereo bytes**, not a universal UDP limit. Historical RPT is **2,223,424 B**; new block transports adapt RRF/RRB, not RPT. RRB32 Q8 edges remain **263,842 B**, and dense noise is about **2.21 MB**. All captured pairs fitting does not remove these rejects. Incremental overflow aborts the whole pair; emitted partial work does not count as a submission. A different cap, fallback, quality profile or codec is an explicit arm with bounded memory and cadence accounting.

At 90 Hz the historical RPT size alone implies **1.601 Gbit/s payload**, before framing/security/repair. User-confirmed **NUC 12 Extreme i7 → 2.5 GbE → ASUS RT-AXE7800** removes a hypothetical 1 GbE host bottleneck. It does not measure the 6 GHz radio's effective service, retries or queue tails. The stated root method and network setup are user-declared configuration, not locally collected device receipts.

**Freshness and cadence:** known-source receipt age is headset-local earliest-known receipt, not calibrated host-to-display source age. Re-encoding cannot reset its provenance. Run05 p95 is **26.927659 ms encoded / 27.037346 ms known-source receipt age**. The fixed full-window distinct-source encoded-age optimum is **15.924690 ms**; the source-age optimum is only bracketed **[15.924690, 15.998856] ms**. The conditional 14.974637 ms unrestricted encoded witness retains repeated sources and hindsight. Endpoint analysis covers five phases and leaves intermediate intervals unresolved. None proves a global impossibility for earlier source/transport arrivals or qualifies a policy.

**Qualification scope:** compositor retains a historical plot-script hash gap; transport retains historical snapshot differences; GPU current-code and historical receipts have distinct source pins. Catalog totals, repeated corpora and inherited/cherry-picked contributions are not independent replications. ## Next experiments and where learning could help

1. **Freeze and reproduce the nonlearned controls.** Bind exact candidate/baseline/harness/binary/configuration/data hashes. Preserve original-input versus previously decoded Q8 semantics. Keep the 12 reused captured pairs as regression data; add independent scenes/sessions before generalization claims.
2. **Test a small exact-reconstruction encoder decision model first.** On a fixed RRB32 profile, predict raw/plain/up decisions from causally available pre-compression features. Compare fixed policies, existing try-both compression and raw fallback. Measure feature/inference/encoding cost, actual bytes, tails and every cap rejection. Test RRB32/128 selection separately.
3. **Integrate one controlled pipeline change.** Measure mapped input through the renderer's last read against matching staged controls; separately test actual worker-to-socket-to-decoder progress. Preserve owners, authenticated session/configuration, deadlines and atomic stereo publication. Never combine unrelated stage medians into a latency claim.
4. **Run protocol comparisons on the declared route.** Keep same-route TCP, historical ADB reverse, secured paced UDP/VXF, QUIC DATAGRAM plus reliable control and RTP/SRTP distinct. Compare FEC/repair at equal budgets under measured wire traces; record PMTU, queues, real arrivals, source age/clock uncertainty, throughput and complete-stereo cadence. Reliable/partially reliable and USB arms remain diagnostic or capability-gated.
5. **Add per-eye allocation only with usable causal traces.** Retain shared-gaze, independent-current-gaze and independent-predicted-gaze arms. The declared `quest-pro-decoupled-eye-tracking-v1` remains assumed until an observed API/capture receipt. Preserve each eye's clock/calibration/validity/confidence/horizon/uncertainty/fallback and one presentation target; maintain coherent stereo/source/warp identity. Changed foveation/quality is a separate arm. Future gaze may be labels, never inputs; freeze scene/session splits and participant separation where relevant.
6. **Then evaluate a small intra-only learned bitstream if the tradeoff warrants it.** Use actual independently decodable bytes plus declared model/configuration, bounded chunks and separate quality/rate/cost curves against VR444, RRF and RRB. An ordinary lossy autoencoder is not exact-Q8 compression. Keep architectures replaceable; no training job or architecture choice is made here.
7. **Use an offline ranker in shadow mode.** Organize proposed measurements now; train only after sufficient independent compatible data. Compare deterministic/round-robin selection, keep evidence domains separate and report uncertainty. It must never fill absent headset metrics, convert bounds into observations or authorize promotion.
